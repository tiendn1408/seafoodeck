Mode: Operate (learner app shell for every learner screen; brand lives in precise details).

## Direction contract

THESIS: "Biển sâu × Ấn bản loài". The learner works a fishing boat's day: each session is a haul (mẻ lưới) pulled from deep water. The shell refuses the generic SaaS quiz frame: no sidebar, no white top bar with grey links, no eyebrow labels, no purple or neon.

OWN-WORLD: Deep surfaces run ink-blue to deep-sea teal with slow light rays; reading surfaces are cold ice-white (never cream or paper). Seafood-flesh group colours: tuna red fish, cooked-shrimp pink crustaceans, clam-flesh ochre molluscs, sea-cucumber grey-green other, blue-grey for no group. Salmon orange is the single action colour, on a pressed button with a darker bottom lip. Fish-scale arcs are decoration only (header bottom edge, deep-floor fade, empty states), never an image frame or mask. Progress is a float rope. Be Vietnam Pro, heavy and tight in headings; scientific names italic; tabular numerals.

STORY: The learner sees today's haul, presses one button to cast, works a few cards on the phone, and comes back when reviews are due. The customer believes this is a seafood-trade product, not a quiz template.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop ≥900px: a 64px deep-sea header, logo with scale-arc mark plus internal-release chip at left, tabs Học / Thư viện / Ôn tập / Tiến độ centred with a salmon underline on the active tab, theme toggle and Đăng xuất at right; transparent over deep screens, solid deep-sea over ice screens. Phone: compact header (logo, toggle, logout) and a fixed four-item bottom tab bar respecting the safe area. The page's primary action belongs to the screen, never the shell.

FORM: User-chosen direction A "Biển sâu" with C "Ấn bản loài" for study screens (approved in spec section 2); not rolled. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
